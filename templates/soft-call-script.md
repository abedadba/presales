# Soft Call Script — {{company_name}}

**Contact:** {{contact_name}}, {{contact_title}}
**Owner:** {{owner_name}} · **Status:** {{lead_status}} · **Date:** {{interaction_date}}

## 1. Opener (30 seconds)

"Hi {{contact_name}}, it's {{owner_name}}. Thanks for your time — I work with {{industry}} teams like {{company_name}} on {{pain_point_1}} and {{pain_point_2}}. The goal of this short call is just to understand whether we can help, and to agree a sensible next step. Is it OK to ask a few questions?"

## 2. Discovery questions

- "What's the biggest challenge you're facing with pain point 1 right now?"
- "How are you handling pain point 2 today — tools, people, process?"
- "If that problem stayed unresolved for another quarter, what would it cost the company?"
- "Who else is involved in a decision like this, besides yourself?"

## 3. Confirm fit

- Summarise back what was heard in 2-3 sentences.
- "Does that sound like a fair summary?"
- "Is it OK if I share a couple of relevant examples from similar companies?"

## 4. Close

- Propose a concrete next step: {{next_step}}
- "Would you be open to a follow-up on {{meeting_date}} to walk through options?"
- "Thanks — I'll send a short summary today."

## Call notes

- Pain point 1 confirmed: {{pain_point_1}}
- Pain point 2 confirmed: {{pain_point_2}}
- Next step: {{next_step}}
